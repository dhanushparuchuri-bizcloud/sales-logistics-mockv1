Quote Construction

Outlook → Outlook Plugin → WorldTrak (creates quote skeleton).
Tariff: lookup rate on file.
Non-tariff (65–75%): 7L lookup, email carrier, or real-time platform (expedite/hotshot).

Portal-originated quotes: data flows directly to WT — fewer app switches, but margin work is the same.

The four headline pains

Anything on the dashboard that doesn't trace to one of these four — or their immediate proof — gets cut. All four apply to both channels.
P8 · VERY HIGH
No proactive follow-up — quotes die in silence
"Main complaint from management is operators don't or don't know when to proactively follow up."
Surface: stale-quote alarms + Nudge action + response-time/win-rate proof. Works on both channels (email silence + portal-view silence)
P13 · VERY HIGH
No win/loss reason recorded
"When a quote is lost, do they record it anywhere? No. That's why we're building this dashboard."
Surface: postmortem on lost quotes — AI extracts from emails. Portal abandonments use form-step + view-pattern as proxy.
P15 · VERY HIGH
Sales has zero visibility
"Sales rep has no visibility into how and what is handled. It's always he said / she said."
Surface: the entire dashboard — every level traces to source emails or portal events.
P16 · VERY HIGH
Operator quality variance — known but unquantified
"The good ones are on top, the bad ones are not. Like any other business."
Surface: operator scorecard scoped to the Sales rep's book — response time, win rate, follow-up rate, unanswered count.
